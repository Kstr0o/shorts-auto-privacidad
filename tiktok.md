# Daily Streamer Clips Uploader – TikTok

[Home](./) · [Privacy Policy](#privacy-policy) · [Terms of Service](#terms-of-service)

**Daily Streamer Clips Uploader** is a private tool used only by its owner. It sends the short clips he edits to his own TikTok account as drafts (Content Posting API, "Upload to TikTok"), so he can review and publish them himself from the TikTok app. It has no public users and no sign-up.

## Privacy Policy

**Data accessed:** only the owner's own TikTok account, which he expressly authorizes through TikTok Login Kit: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload videos as drafts (`video.upload`).

**Data not collected:** the tool does not access data of other people, does not collect viewer data, does not use cookies and does not sell or share any information with third parties.

**Storage:** the access token is stored encrypted as a private secret of the owner's repository and is used only to upload his own drafts.

**Revoking access:** access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions). To ask for stored data to be deleted, open an issue at [https://github.com/Kstr0o/shorts-auto-privacidad/issues](https://github.com/Kstr0o/shorts-auto-privacidad/issues).
