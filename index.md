# shorts-auto

**shorts-auto** is a private tool used only by its owner to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels and to check the statistics of those channels. It has no public users and no sign-up.

## Privacy Policy

shorts-auto uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). See also the [Google Privacy Policy](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

**Data accessed:** only the YouTube channels of the owner, which he expressly authorizes (video upload, channel information and YouTube Analytics statistics of those channels).

**Data not collected:** it does not access data of other people, does not collect viewer data, does not use cookies, and does not sell or share any information with third parties.

**Storage:** access credentials are stored encrypted as private secrets of the owner's repository and are used only for these functions. Statistics obtained from the API are refreshed regularly and are not kept for more than 30 days without being updated.

**Revoking access:** access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). To ask for stored data to be deleted, open an issue at [https://github.com/Kstr0o/shorts-auto-privacidad/issues](https://github.com/Kstr0o/shorts-auto-privacidad/issues).

**Use of Google data:** the use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Política de privacidad

shorts-auto es una herramienta privada que solo usa su propietario para publicar vídeos en sus propios canales de YouTube y consultar las estadísticas de esos canales. Usa los Servicios de la API de YouTube; al usarla, el propietario acepta las [Condiciones del Servicio de YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Consulta también la [Política de Privacidad de Google](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

**Datos a los que accede:** únicamente los canales de YouTube del propietario que este autoriza de forma expresa (subida de vídeos, información del canal y estadísticas de YouTube Analytics).

**Datos que no recoge:** no accede a datos de otras personas, no recoge datos de espectadores, no usa cookies y no vende ni comparte ninguna información con terceros.

**Almacenamiento:** las credenciales de acceso se guardan cifradas como secretos privados del repositorio del propietario y solo sirven para estas funciones. Se pueden revocar en cualquier momento desde [https://myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions)

**Uso de datos de Google:** el uso de la información recibida de las APIs de Google cumple la Política de Datos de Usuario de los Servicios de las APIs de Google, incluidos los requisitos de uso limitado.
